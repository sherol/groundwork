Groundwork

Self-guided tools for a steadier body and mind.

Four practices that work well together: settle the body, untangle hard thoughts, ride out strong emotions, and care for your gut. Pick one to start. Your progress stays in this browser, and can sync to one Google Sheet.

1 · Body

Somatic Experiencing Method

A 12-phase program for steadying your nervous system through body sensation, following Peter Levine's healing trauma program.

  • Lessons, guided exercises and audio
  • Before/after activation ratings
  • One-page therapist report
 2 · Mind

Thought Records & Cognitive Distortions

Catch a painful thought, spot the thinking traps in it, weigh the evidence, and find a more balanced view.

  • 12 common thinking traps, with examples
  • Step-by-step thought record
  • Saved records to share with your therapist
 3 · Emotions

DBT Skills

Dialectical Behavior Therapy skills in four modules: mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation and interpersonal effectiveness.

  • Every core skill in plain language
  • "What do I need right now?" skill finder
  • Daily diary card
 4 · Gut

Integrative Eating

Calm, mindful meals and gut health: breathe before you eat, chew each bite 30–50 times, build fiber slowly, and be your own private investigator.

  • How digestion works, from mouth to gut
  • Acid, enzymes and elimination diet trials
  • Pre-meal breathing and weekly homework
  • Daily food, fiber and symptom log

Groundwork is a self-regulation and learning tool, not therapy, medical advice or a substitute for care. Use it alongside your therapist, doctor or care team. Go slowly and stop whenever you want. About the 12 somatic phases.

Everything you enter stays in this browser on this device. Connect Google Sheets (top of any page) to keep all four sections in one spreadsheet, Groundwork progress, in your own Google Drive, and to use them on another device. Groundwork can only see files it created, never the rest of your Drive.
