Groundwork is a self-regulation and learning tool, not therapy or a substitute for care. It is inspired by body-based trauma approaches, including Peter Levine's work, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Somatic Experiencing International. The exercises stay with present-moment body sensation; they never ask you to revisit a traumatic memory. Go slowly, stop whenever you want, and do deeper work with a trained practitioner. In the US, call or text 988 any time you're in crisis.
