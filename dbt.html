DBT Skills

Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) teaches practical skills for staying present, getting through a crisis without making it worse, steadying strong emotions, and asking for what you need.

"Dialectical" means holding two things at once: I accept myself as I am, and I'm working to change. Both are true.
